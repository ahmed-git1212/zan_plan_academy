About ZPA

A focused A‑Level home
in Fuoni Migombani.

Zan Plan Academy is a community-minded secondary school offering Form Five and Form Six studies with a strong emphasis on academic readiness, science practicals and personal growth.

Our learners are known for their discipline, curiosity and ambition. Teachers work in small teams, track progress closely, and make room for every student to ask questions, practise and improve.

  • Advanced Physics, Chemistry and Biology laboratories
  • Small-group mentoring and regular progress reviews
  • University and career guidance from Form Five
  • A safe, values-led campus close to home

Form Five admission

Your future starts here.

Admission enquiries are open for the 2026/2027 Form Five intake at Fuoni Migombani.

What you will need

  • Form Four (CSEE) results or provisional results
  • Relevant subject passes for your chosen stream
  • Birth certificate or student identification
  • Two recent passport-size photographs

How to apply

  1. 01 Visit the Fuoni Migombani campus or call admissions.
  2. 02 Choose a subject combination with guidance from our team.
  3. 03 Submit your results and documents for review.

Admission dates and requirements are confirmed by the admissions office. Please call or visit us for the current intake timetable.
